## 📌 Executive Summary
An interactive and dynamic **Power BI Sales Performance Dashboard** designed to analyze sales, profit margins, and volume across multiple global regions. The dashboard provides actionable business insights for sales managers and operational leadership to track KPIs, top-performing products, and geographic distribution.

---

## 🎯 Key Features
* **Geographic Spatial Analysis:** Interactive Map visual pinpointing sales hotspots across global markets (India, USA, UK, Germany, China, Japan) with bubble-size scaling.
* **Executive KPI Panel:** Dynamic metric cards tracking **Total Sales**, **Total Profit**, and **Units Sold**.
* **Product & Category Performance:** Horizontal Bar Chart breaking down **Top 5 Selling Products** and Donut Chart analyzing **Category Share** (Electronics, Furniture, Clothing, Groceries).
* **Interactive Slicers:** Country-level dynamic filtering enabling real-time drill-down capabilities.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools Used
* **Business Intelligence Tool:** Power BI Desktop
* **Data Transformation:** Power Query (Data cleaning, data type assignments, geographic categorization)
* **Data Modeling & Calculations:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for `Total Sales`, `Total Profit`, and `Units Sold` aggregations
* **Data Source:** Custom Financial & Sales Dataset (`.xlsx`)

---

## Key Business Insights
1. **Top Revenue Contributor:** **Laptop Pro** generated the highest sales revenue across regions, making Electronics the primary growth driver.
2. **Geographic Hotspots:** India and the United States represent the highest transaction volume and profitability.
3. **Category Breakdown:** Electronics holds over **40%+** share of total sales, followed by Furniture and Clothing.
